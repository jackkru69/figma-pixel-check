Generated by `node test/bench.mjs`. Each mutation is a realistic mistake injected into a finished corpus screen.
**Detected**: its own section shows a changed Δ top / Δ height, a new spacing flag, a mismatch ≥ 1 pp higher,
or a colour share over the report's 0.5 % mark. **Weak**: only a smaller signal there (mismatch ≥ 0.1 pp,
colour ≥ 0.05 pp, a spacing value below the flag). **Misattributed**: a
signal only in other sections. **Missed**: nothing. Invalid mutations (the capture did not change) are left out.
